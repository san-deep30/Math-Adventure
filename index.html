Learn four maths superpowers

Understand what each operation means, then practise with fresh questions.

Addition

Put numbers together to find how many there are altogether. Learn carrying with clear place-value steps.

➖

Subtraction

Take one number away from another. Understand borrowing and discover what is left.

45 − 12 = 33
✖️

Multiplication

Make equal groups and use repeated addition to find the total quickly.

4 × 3 = 12
➗

Division

Share objects equally into groups and learn how multiplication helps us check.

12 ÷ 3 = 4
